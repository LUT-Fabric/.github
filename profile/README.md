## LUT Fabric
> Learn designing complete digital systems.

## What is LUT Fabric?
LUT Fabric is a hands-on learning platform for VHDL and FPGA development running locally on your system.
Through structured courses, you'll build hardware modules one task at a time, simulate and verify your designs using GHDL, and combine your modules into complete systems.
From fundamental digital logic to advanced AXI interfaces and video-processing pipelines, LUT Fabric helps you develop practical skills and understand how real digital systems are designed.

Its focus is on designing complete digital systems rather than simply learning VHDL syntax.

## How does it work?
LUT Fabric is an open, self-hosted learning platform that runs in a Docker container on your own machine or server.
It serves a web application that lets you explore courses, complete tasks, and simulate your VHDL designs without relying on a hosted service or the hassle of installing the full tool-chain yourself.

**Learn your way.**
LUT Fabric automatically loads official courses from its [public course repository](https://github.com/LUT-Fabric/courses).
You can contribute to these courses, add your own local courses through the web interface, or connect third-party course repositories.

**Make it your own.**
Because LUT Fabric is open source, anyone can adapt it to their needs.
For example, universities can maintain their own official course repository and distribute a customized version of the platform to their students.

**Keep your progress.**
Export and import your work to preserve your progress across container instances, or configure a Git repository to store your work automatically.
Submitted solutions are committed to your repository, giving you a persistent, version-controlled record of your progress.
